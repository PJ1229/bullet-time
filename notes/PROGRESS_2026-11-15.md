## Learnings
- Image-to-3D with TRELLIS (free Hugging Face demo) turns a flat goalie cutout into a usable `.glb`, so an orbit shot reads as 3D instead of a card. It needs a clean transparent PNG going in.
- Other free image-to-3D options: Hunyuan3D-2 (better textures) and Stable Fast 3D (fastest).
- Version every render into its own `render_bullettime_vNN_<look>` folder so looks can be compared side by side.
- On this 18 GB Mac, don't run Resolve, Blender and Claude's virtual machine together with parallel ffmpeg jobs; that's what kept causing the out-of-memory errors.
